Specifying hand-tufted

When to specify
hand-tufted.

Specify hand-tufting for carved outlines, sculpted relief, mixed pile heights and loop-and-cut surfaces — or where the programme needs a shorter lead time than hand-knotting allows.

Yarn and sampling

Yarn, colour
and specification.

Pile yarns are selected per design from New Zealand wool, hand-spun wool, wool-silk blends, bamboo silk and developed blends; pile heights and carving are drawn into the specification. Yarn, spin and dye lot are approved in the hand against your scheme, and a woven strike-off — ready in 2–3 weeks once the design is approved — is signed off before the full rug is set on the loom. Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks, up to six months in some cases. There is no minimum order. Rug materials · The process · Brief a hand-tufted rug
