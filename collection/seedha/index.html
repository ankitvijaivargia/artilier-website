The construction

About flatweave rugs.

Woven flat and low underfoot. Flatweave reads graphically, suits layering and rooms where a pile would sit too high, and is lighter than a pile rug. Which construction suits a design is decided from the artwork, the detail it carries, the traffic the room will take and the budget — see the craft and materials, or start from your own artwork.
