Hand-knotted rug

Anhad

A classical floral field on a black ground, framed by a deep border in ivory and gold. Hand-knotted, and built for rooms that can carry a dark, formal floor.

Hand-knottedAny dimensionCustom colourways

Specification

ConstructionHand-knotted
PatternClassical floral field with a deep border
Colours shownBlack · Ivory · Gold — re-colourable to your scheme
Yarn optionsNew Zealand wool, hand-spun wool, wool-silk blend, bamboo silk or a developed blend — specified per project
SizeAny dimension — made to order
SamplingStrike-off in 2–3 weeks once the design is approved; its cost is credited against the order
Lead timeUsually 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval; up to six months in some cases
Minimum orderNone — a single rug is welcome

Anhad is a starting point rather than a fixed product. It can be re-scaled, re-proportioned and re-coloured against your scheme, and woven to the dimensions the room needs. Yarn, colour and construction are confirmed in sampling before a loom is set.

The construction

About hand-knotted rugs.

Knotted by hand on the loom. Hand-knotting gives the finest definition the studio offers and carries painterly artwork best; it also has the longest lead time of the three constructions. Which construction suits a design is decided from the artwork, the detail it carries, the traffic the room will take and the budget — see the craft and materials, or start from your own artwork.
