Artilier/The collection
The collection

Named designs,
made to your dimensions.

The first Artilier collection is rugs. Designs developed in the studio, held across our three main constructions and a further group of other techniques. Each one is a starting point rather than a fixed product: any design can be woven in another size, another proportion, another colourway — or adapted until it belongs to your scheme. The collection grows as new designs are developed.

45 designsAny dimensionCustom colourways
Collection 01

Hand-knotted

Knot by knot on the loom.

The finest definition we can offer, and the construction that carries painterly artwork best. Knot count, yarn and dye lot are set per design.

15 designs shown. Every one is woven to order — re-coloured, re-scaled and re-proportioned for the project. New designs are added as the studio develops them.

Collection 02

Hand-tufted

Texture, carving and pile height.

Carved outlines, loop-and-cut surfaces and high-low relief, at a shorter lead time than knotting.

12 designs shown. Every one is woven to order — re-coloured, re-scaled and re-proportioned for the project. New designs are added as the studio develops them.

Collection 03

Flatweave

Woven flat, low underfoot.

Graphic and geometric work, layering, and rooms where a pile would sit too high.

10 designs shown. Every one is woven to order — re-coloured, re-scaled and re-proportioned for the project. New designs are added as the studio develops them.

Collection 04

Other techniques

Constructions we develop beyond the three main ones.

Printed, braided, soumak, loop-and-cut and mixed constructions — developed where a design or an application calls for them. Availability depends on the design, the size and the workshop, so these are shown as work rather than as a standing range.

8 designs shown, and the group grows as new techniques are developed. Every one is woven to order — re-coloured, re-scaled and re-proportioned for the project.
