Specifying flatweave

When to specify
flatweave.

Specify flatweave for graphic and geometric work, for layering, and for rooms where a pile would sit too high. It is lower and lighter underfoot than a pile rug, and reversible in some constructions.

Yarn and sampling

Yarn, colour
and specification.

Flatweaves are specified in cotton, jute, allo / hemp and developed blends, chosen for the surface the room needs. Yarn, spin and dye lot are approved in the hand against your scheme, and a woven strike-off — ready in 2–3 weeks once the design is approved — is signed off before the full rug is set on the loom. Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval, up to six months in some cases. There is no minimum order. Rug materials · The process · Brief a flatweave rug
