Specifying hand-knotted

When to specify
hand-knotted.

Specify hand-knotting when the artwork carries fine detail, tonal gradation or painterly marks, or when the rug is the focal piece of a formal room. It holds the finest definition of the three constructions, and it has the longest lead time — plan the programme around it.

Yarn and sampling

Yarn, colour
and specification.

Pile yarns are selected per design from New Zealand wool, hand-spun wool, wool-silk blends, bamboo silk and developed blends; knot count is set to the detail the artwork needs. Yarn, spin and dye lot are approved in the hand against your scheme, and a woven strike-off — ready in 2–3 weeks once the design is approved — is signed off before the full rug is set on the loom. Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval, up to six months in some cases. There is no minimum order. Rug materials · The process · Brief a hand-knotted rug
