Bhanwar

A soft rose and beige classical floral, even across the field. Hand-tufted.

Specification

ConstructionHand-tufted
PatternAll-over classical floral
Colours shownSoft rose · Beige — re-colourable to your scheme
Yarn optionsNew Zealand wool, hand-spun wool, wool-silk blend, bamboo silk or a developed blend — specified per project
SizeMade to order and sized to the project; limits for the construction confirmed before sampling
SamplingStrike-off in 2–3 weeks once the design is approved; its cost is credited against the order

Minimum orderNone — a single rug is welcome

Bhanwar is a starting point rather than a fixed product. It can be re-scaled, re-proportioned and re-coloured against your scheme, and woven to the dimensions the room needs. Yarn, colour and construction are confirmed in sampling before a loom is set.

The construction

About hand-tufted rugs.

Tufted to the drawing. Hand-tufting allows carving, mixed pile heights and loop-and-cut surfaces, at a shorter lead time than hand-knotting. Which construction suits a design is decided from the artwork, the detail it carries, the traffic the room will take and the budget — see the craft and materials, or start from your own artwork.
