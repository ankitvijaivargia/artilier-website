Before you write

Questions we are often asked.

Do you sell to private clients?
Not directly. Artilier works trade only — with interior designers, architects, design practices and galleries, and with collectors through the galleries that represent them.
Can a collection design be changed?
Yes. Every design is a starting point: it can be re-scaled, re-proportioned and re-coloured to your scheme, and made to the size the room needs within what the design and construction allow.
Can we see samples first?
Yes. For rugs, yarn samples, colour trials and a woven strike-off are approved in the hand before the full rug is set on the loom. Where a strike-off is part of the project, it typically takes 2–3 weeks once the design or sketch is approved, and its cost is credited against the order.
Is there a minimum order?
Trade only. No fixed minimum order quantity — each project is assessed on its design, technical requirements and production specifications.
How are prices quoted?
Prices are quoted in USD or EUR, usually ex-works; FOB or DDP can be quoted separately on request. Payment terms are set out in the quote, and rugs are usually shipped rolled.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Artilier is its trade studio — one supplier and one point of coordination.
How long does it take?
Rugs: usually 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval, and up to six months in some cases; hand-knotting takes longest, hand-tufting is shorter. Embroidered wall panels and upholstered headboards: production typically takes 3–4 months, depending on the design, specifications and project requirements; more complex projects may require up to 6 months. Embroidered fabric: typically 6–8 weeks; complex work up to 6 months. We confirm delivery dates only after reviewing the specification.
