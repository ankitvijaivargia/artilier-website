Before you write

Questions we are often asked.

Do you sell to private clients?
No. Artilier works trade only — with interior designers, architects, design practices and the hospitality teams who specify with them.
Can a collection design be changed?
Yes. Every design is a starting point: it can be re-scaled, re-proportioned and re-coloured to your scheme, and woven to any dimension.
Can we see samples first?
Yes. Yarn samples, colour trials and a woven strike-off are approved in the hand before the full rug is set on the loom. The strike-off takes 2–3 weeks once the design or sketch is approved, and its cost is credited against the order.
Is there a minimum order?
No. We make single pieces as readily as multi-rug schedules.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Artilier is its trade studio — one supplier and one point of coordination.
How long does it take?
Usually 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval, and up to six months in some cases; we confirm the timeline for each project. Hand-knotting takes longest; hand-tufting is shorter.
