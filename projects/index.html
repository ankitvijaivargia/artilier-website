Being documented

Artilier is a new studio. Installed projects are published here only once they are photographed and the designers involved have agreed to what is shown — nothing on this page will be staged or invented.

Until then, the clearest view of our work is the collection and how we develop rugs from a designer’s artwork. If you would like to discuss work relevant to your project, write to us.
