Techniques

The stitch the
design asks for.

These are the techniques our embroiderers work in most, often combined in one piece. Which a design wants is decided from the artwork, the ground fabric and where the piece will live — and proved in the strike-off. Other techniques on request.

01ZardoziMetal threads, wires and coiled purls laid and couched by hand. Raised, reflective relief for panels and framed pieces.
02ZariFine metallic thread worked flat for line, outline and fill — on its own, or with silk and zardozi for contrast between flat and raised.
03AariChain stitch drawn with a hooked needle on the frame, and on hand-guided chain-stitch machines for larger fields and looped, chenille-like texture.
04RaffiaRaffia and natural cord coiled, couched and stitched onto the ground. Heavy, graphic texture for wall panels and headboards.
05Hand embroiderySatin, long-and-short, couching and knot stitches in silk, cotton or wool, for shading, fine detail and large worked grounds.
06Appliqué and loop workCut fabric laid onto the ground and stitched down, combined with raised loops and couched line — sculptural, tonal surfaces for wall panels.

Wall to wall

Hand-embroidered
wallcoverings.

Embroidery can cover a whole wall, not only a panel on it. Send the elevations with doors, windows and switches marked; we lay out the panels and their joins with you, so the design runs across the wall as one surface, and prove the stitch, ground and colour in a strike-off before production.

From your artwork

Your drawing,
in thread.

Embroidery is commissioned the way our rugs are. Send artwork, a painting, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes; we resolve technique, thread, colour and ground fabric with you, then prove it in a strike-off before production.

01BriefThe artwork or reference, the piece and its size, the fabric if one is already specified, and the date it is needed on site.
02DevelopmentTechnique, stitch density, thread and colour are worked out against the artwork in our New Delhi studio.
03Strike-offA worked sample for approval in the hand, usually 2–3 weeks after the design is approved. Its cost is credited against the order.
04ProductionEmbroidered by hand in established workshops across India, then checked in Delhi against the approved strike-off before it leaves.

Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

Is there a minimum order?
No. A single cushion or panel is made as readily as a full scheme.
Can you embroider a whole wall?
Yes. Embroidered wallcoverings are made as panels laid out to your elevations and fitted edge to edge. Mark doors, windows and switches on the drawings; the layout and joins are agreed with you before the strike-off.
How long does it take?
The strike-off usually takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved. Production time depends on technique, density and area, and is confirmed for each piece in the quote.
Can embroidery be paired with a rug?
Yes. Rugs and embroidery are developed in the same studio, so colour and motif can be carried across both for one room.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs.
