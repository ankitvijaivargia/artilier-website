Artilier/Embroidery
Embroidery · Drawn in thread

Embroidered wall panels,
made for the room.

Artilier develops, makes and supplies bespoke embroidered wall panels for interior designers and architects working on residential and hospitality interiors — a single framed piece, a run of panels set into joinery, or panels planned across a room from wall to wall. Each design starts from a drawing, ours or yours. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.

The work

A single wall,
or a whole room.

Each piece is worked on the frame in our New Delhi studio and in the established workshops we work with across India, then checked in Delhi before it leaves. Embroidery is also made for headboards, upholstery and curtains — see embroidered headboards and embroidered fabric by the metre.

Techniques

The stitch the
design asks for.

These are the techniques our embroiderers work in most, often combined on one ground. We recommend the technique once we have seen the design, the scale and how close the work will be viewed. Other techniques on request.

01ZardoziMetal threads, wires and coiled purls laid and couched by hand. Raised, reflective relief for panels and framed pieces.
02ZariFine metallic thread worked flat for line, outline and fill — on its own, or with silk and zardozi for contrast between flat and raised.
03AariChain stitch drawn with a hooked needle on the frame, and on hand-guided chain-stitch machines for larger fields and looped, chenille-like texture.
04RaffiaRaffia and natural cord coiled, couched and stitched onto the ground. Heavy, graphic texture for wall panels and headboards.
05Hand embroiderySatin, long-and-short, couching and knot stitches in silk, cotton or wool, for shading, fine detail and large worked grounds.
06Appliqué and loop workCut fabric laid onto the ground and stitched down, combined with raised loops and couched line — sculptural, tonal surfaces for wall panels.

Construction and dimensions

Fabric-only
or on board.

Construction and dimensions are set for each project. Depending on the specification, panels are supplied as a single piece or in sections planned for discreet joins. Fabric-only panels can be folded for shipping where the embroidery and material allow. For panels mounted on MDF or timber board, the standard maximum we currently confirm is 8 ft × 4 ft (approximately 244 × 122 cm) per panel; larger boards need a feasibility check with our supplier. Final dimensions are agreed with the project team once the design, construction and shipping requirements have been reviewed.

The ground is chosen with the embroidery. Its suitability depends on the technique and stitch density, on whether the panel is supplied fabric-only or mounted on board, and on the project’s requirements. If you have already specified a ground, send it with the brief and we will confirm whether it suits the work, or propose an alternative in development.

From drawing to dispatch

Your drawing,
in thread.

Send artwork, a painting, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes. We resolve technique, thread, colour and ground with you before anything is costed in detail.

01BriefDrawing, elevation or reference; wall dimensions; ground preference; viewing distance; fabric-only or board-mounted; delivery country; who will install.
02DevelopmentTechnique, stitch density, thread and colour are worked out against the artwork in our New Delhi studio.
03Strike-offWhere a strike-off is part of the project, we make a worked sample of a representative section for your approval before production. It typically takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved, and its cost is credited against the order. Production time is separate.
04ProductionProduction typically takes 3–4 months, depending on the design, specifications and project requirements. More complex projects may require up to 6 months. We confirm a delivery date only after reviewing the specification. Each piece is checked in Delhi before it leaves.
05DispatchPacked to suit the construction and dispatched as agreed in the quote. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.
Quantity and specification

One panel
or a scheme.

No fixed minimum order quantity. Each project is assessed according to its design, technical requirements and production specifications.

Project-specific documentation and testing requirements can be discussed during specification. Any required standards and supporting evidence must be assessed and agreed before the order is accepted. Where a project requires testing or documentation, we will tell you before the order is accepted whether it can be arranged for that product and within that timeline.

Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

Do you install the panels?
No. We make and supply the panels; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor.
Can you embroider a whole wall?
Yes. Embroidered wallcoverings are supplied as panels laid out to your elevations, ready for your installer to fit edge to edge. Mark doors, windows and switches on the drawings; the layout and joins are agreed with you before production.
How visible are the joins?
Joins are planned to sit discreetly within the design. How visible they are depends on the construction, the ground and the embroidery, so we discuss it with you before production rather than promise it.
How large can a panel be?
For panels mounted on MDF or timber board, the standard maximum we currently confirm is 8 ft × 4 ft (approximately 244 × 122 cm) per panel; larger boards need a feasibility check with our supplier. Fabric-only panels are not bound by that board size, and final dimensions are agreed once the design, construction and shipping requirements have been reviewed.
How long does it take?
Production typically takes 3–4 months, depending on the design, specifications and project requirements. More complex projects may require up to 6 months. We confirm a delivery date only after reviewing the specification. Where a strike-off is part of the project, it typically takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved.
Is there a minimum order?
No fixed minimum order quantity. Each project is assessed according to its design, technical requirements and production specifications.
Can embroidery be paired with a rug?
Yes. Rugs and embroidery are developed in the same studio, so colour and motif can be carried across both for one room.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs.
