Hand embroidery for interiors: wall panels, framed art, headboards, upholstery panels, cushions and drapery. Worked from our designs or developed from your own artwork, sampled for approval, and made to the piece the room needs.

Techniques

Every stitch the
design asks for.

We work across hand-embroidery techniques rather than in one house style. Which technique a piece wants is decided from the artwork, the ground fabric and where the piece will live — and proved in the strike-off.

01AariA fine hooked needle drawing a continuous chain stitch across fabric stretched on a frame. Fast, fluid line work and dense fields of colour.
02Zardozi and metal threadMetallic threads, wires and purls laid and couched by hand. Raised, reflective surfaces for panels and framed pieces.
03CrewelWool yarn on cotton or linen. Soft, textured line and fill, suited to upholstery, headboards and drapery.
04AppliquéCut fabric laid onto a ground and stitched in place. Large, graphic forms at a scale thread alone would take far longer to fill.
05Beadwork and sequinsBeads, sequins and stones worked by hand for light, depth and relief — used across a whole surface or as an accent.
06Thread embroiderySatin, stem, long-and-short and knot stitches in silk, cotton or wool, for painterly shading and fine detail. Techniques are combined where a design needs it.

Where it is used

Made for the wall,
the bed and the window.

Tell us what the piece is and where it goes. Size, ground fabric, how it will be mounted or upholstered and how much handling it will take all shape the technique and density we recommend.

Wall panelsSingle panels or runs across a wall, sized to the elevation
Framed artEmbroidered pieces made to be framed or mounted
HeadboardsEmbroidered panels for upholstered headboards
Upholstery panelsSeat, back and side panels for upholstered furniture
CushionsEmbroidered fronts, made to size
DraperyEmbroidered curtains, leading edges and borders
From your artwork

Your drawing,
in thread.

Embroidery is commissioned the way our rugs are. Send artwork, a painting, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes; we resolve technique, thread, colour and ground fabric with you, then prove it in a strike-off before production.

01BriefThe artwork or reference, the piece and its size, the fabric if one is already specified, and the date it is needed on site.
02DevelopmentTechnique, stitch density, thread and colour are worked out against the artwork in our New Delhi studio.
03Strike-offA worked sample for approval in the hand, usually 2–3 weeks after the design is approved. Its cost is credited against the order.
04ProductionEmbroidered by hand in established workshops across India, then checked in Delhi against the approved strike-off before it leaves.
Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

Is there a minimum order?
No. A single cushion or panel is made as readily as a full scheme.
How long does it take?
The strike-off usually takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved. Production time depends on technique, density and area, and is confirmed for each piece in the quote.
Can embroidery be paired with a rug?
Yes. Rugs and embroidery are developed in the same studio, so colour and motif can be carried across both for one room.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs.
