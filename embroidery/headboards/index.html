Embroidery · Headboards

Embroidered
headboards.

Bespoke headboards developed from your drawing or from our embroidery, for hotel rooms and private residences. We work with interior designers and hospitality design teams from first drawing to the finished piece.

Two ways to work with us

The whole headboard,
or the panel.

Choose the route that fits how the project is already specified.

Complete headboards

We make the upholstered headboard with the embroidery built in.

Embroidered headboard panels for your upholsterer

We supply the embroidered panel; your upholsterer builds the finished headboard. Useful when the frame, foam or fixing detail is already specified by your joinery or upholstery partner.

Hotel room schedules

One room,
or every room.

No fixed minimum order quantity. Each project is assessed according to its design, technical requirements and production specifications. This applies to complete headboards and to embroidered panels.

Hotel headboards are usually specified against a room schedule, and we can produce repeat headboards for it. Quantities, room types and the production sequence are agreed against the schedule at quotation; where a strike-off is part of the project, it is approved before production begins.

Production typically takes 3–4 months, depending on the design, specifications and project requirements. More complex projects may require up to 6 months. We confirm a delivery date only after reviewing the specification. Where a strike-off is part of the project, it typically takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved. Production time is separate.

Specification and documentation

Agreed before
the order.

Project-specific documentation and testing requirements can be discussed during specification. Any required standards and supporting evidence must be assessed and agreed before the order is accepted. We do not hold product certifications; if your project requires testing, we will confirm before acceptance whether it can be arranged for that headboard and within the programme.

What to send us

A useful
first brief.

  • Elevation or drawing of the headboard, with dimensions
  • Route — complete headboard, or embroidered panel for your upholsterer
  • Quantities by room type, if for a hotel
  • Fabric or ground preference, if one is already specified
  • Delivery country and the target date
  • Specification requirements — any documentation or testing the project needs
