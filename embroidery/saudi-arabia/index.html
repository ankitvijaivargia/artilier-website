Artilier/Embroidery/Saudi Arabia
Embroidery · Saudi Arabia

Hand embroidery for
Saudi projects.

For interior designers, architects and galleries working in Riyadh, Jeddah, AlUla and across the Kingdom: embroidered wall panels, framed pieces, headboards and upholstery panels, made to commission from your scheme or artwork and approved in a strike-off first.

Saudi projectsResidential · hospitality · culturalStrike-off first
Where it is used

Panels, headboards, whole schemes.

  • Residences — wall-to-wall embroidered wallcoverings, majlis wall panels, framed works, headboards and soft furnishings
  • Hospitality — hotels and resorts, including destination projects, usually briefed as a schedule of panel types across many rooms
  • Cultural and gallery projects — single works and series, through the galleries and curators who commission them
  • Furniture — embroidered panels supplied to your upholsterer or joinery workshop
Long programmes

Schedule the strike-off early.

Larger Saudi projects often run on long programmes with many embroidered pieces per building. Send the full schedule as early as you can: strike-offs can then be planned against your mock-up and approval dates rather than squeezed in at the end.

Working together

Two and a half hours apart.

Saudi Arabia (AST, UTC+3) is two hours thirty minutes behind New Delhi (IST, UTC+5:30), and the Saudi working week runs Sunday to Thursday. Briefs, drawings and approvals move by email; the strike-off is approved in the hand before production.

Questions

From Saudi practices.

Is there a minimum order?
No. A single panel or cushion is made as readily as a full scheme.
How long does it take?
The strike-off usually takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved, and its cost is credited against the order. Production time depends on technique, density and area, and is confirmed for each piece in the quote.
Who do you work with?
Interior designers, architects and design practices, and galleries — including collectors through the galleries that represent them. We do not sell retail.
Can embroidery be matched to rugs on the same project?
Yes. Both are developed in our studio, so a scheme can carry across rugs and embroidered panels. See rugs for Saudi projects.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Artilier is its trade studio; orders are contracted, invoiced and exported through that entity, usually quoted ex-works in USD or EUR, with FOB or DDP on request.
