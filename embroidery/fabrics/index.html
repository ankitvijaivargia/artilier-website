Embroidery · Fabric

Embroidered furnishing
fabric.

Embroidered fabric by the metre for upholstery, curtains and soft furnishings, developed for a specific project. Furnishing fabric only — we do not supply dress or apparel fabric.

Upholstery and curtains

For the chair,
the bed and the window.

Embroidered upholstery fabric for seating, cushions and upholstered headboards, and embroidered curtain fabric for curtains and leading edges. Custom embroidered fabric is developed for interior designers on a specific project, from your design or ours.

Grounds

The ground
you specify.

We can consider the ground you specify, provided it supports the embroidery technique. Where it does not, we will say so at the brief and suggest alternatives.

Widths and quantities

By the metre,
to the project.

Usual widths are up to 60 inches; wider can sometimes be made, depending on the design and technique. There is no fixed minimum order quantity. Pricing depends on design, density and quantity.

WidthUsually up to 60 in (approximately 152 cm)
MinimumNo fixed minimum order quantity

UseUpholstery, curtains and soft furnishings
Designs

Scale, colour,
placement.

Designs can be customised for individual projects — scale, colour, density and placement. Repeat and placement are agreed with you before production.

Lead time

Weeks,
not seasons.

Typical lead time is 6–8 weeks, depending on the design, ground and quantity. Complex work may take up to 6 months. Where a strike-off is part of the project, it typically takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved. Production time is separate.

Specification and documentation

Agreed before
the order.

Project-specific documentation and testing requirements can be discussed during specification. Any required standards and supporting evidence must be assessed and agreed before the order is accepted. Embroidered fabric is not supplied as tested or certified unless that has been agreed in writing for the project.

What to send us

A useful
first brief.

  • Design or reference — your drawing, or the embroidery you would like adapted
  • Use — upholstery, curtains or other soft furnishings, and the pieces it is for
  • Quantity in metres, and the width you need
  • Ground, if one is already specified
  • Delivery country and the target date
  • Specification requirements — any documentation or testing the project needs
