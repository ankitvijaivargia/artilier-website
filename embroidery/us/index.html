Embroidery · United States

Hand embroidery for
New York and the US.

For interior designers, architects and galleries in New York and across the United States: embroidered wall panels, headboards, upholstery panels and framed works, developed from your drawing in our New Delhi studio, with a strike-off approved in the hand where the project requires one.

New York & USMade to commissionNo fixed minimum
Where it is used

Apartments, houses, galleries.

  • Apartments and townhouses — hand-embroidered wallcoverings made wall to wall, wall panels, headboards and cushion fronts sized to the room
  • Houses — larger runs of panels for living rooms, bedrooms and stairs
  • Upholstery panels — embroidered panels supplied to your workroom
  • Galleries and collectors — single works and series, through the galleries that represent them
Techniques

Matt or metal, flat or raised.

Zardozi and zari for couched metal thread and raised relief; aari for chain-stitch line and dense fields; raffia and appliqué for heavy, graphic texture; hand embroidery in silk, cotton or wool for shading and fine detail. See embroidery for the full range.

Working together

A brief at the end of your day, read at the start of ours.

New Delhi is nine hours thirty minutes ahead of New York during daylight saving time (ten hours thirty in winter). Work moves by email, and sizes can be sent in inches or centimetres, whichever your drawings use. Where there is a strike-off, it is approved in the hand before production.

Questions

From US practices.

Is there a minimum order?
No fixed minimum order quantity. Each project is assessed according to its design, technical requirements and production specifications.
How long does it take?
For embroidered wall panels and upholstered headboards, production typically takes 3–4 months, depending on the design, specifications and project requirements; more complex projects may require up to 6 months. Fabric by the metre has its own lead time — see embroidered fabric. Where a strike-off is part of the project, it typically takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved, and its cost is credited against the order. We confirm a delivery date only after reviewing the specification.
Do you install the panels?
No. We make and supply embroidered wall panels; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor.
Who do you work with?
Interior designers, architects and design practices, and galleries — including collectors through the galleries that represent them. We do not sell retail.
Can embroidery be matched to rugs in the same room?
Yes. Both are developed in our studio, so colour and motif can carry across. See rugs for US projects.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Artilier is its trade studio; orders are contracted, invoiced and exported through that entity, usually quoted ex-works in USD or EUR, with FOB or DDP on request.
