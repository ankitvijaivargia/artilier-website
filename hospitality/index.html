Hospitality & contract

Rugs and embroidery
for hospitality.

For hospitality designers, architects and procurement teams: custom rugs for hotels and boutique hospitality projects, made to the dimensions of each room — and embroidered headboards, wall panels and furnishing fabric developed for the same scheme in our New Delhi studio.

What we make for hospitality

Custom rugs, not stock.

Every rug is made to order in the construction, material, colourway and dimensions the project needs. Collection designs can be adapted to a scheme, or a rug developed from the designer’s own artwork. See bespoke development.

Embroidery for the same rooms

Headboards, walls and fabric.

Developed in the same studio as the rugs, so colour and motif can carry across a room. Delivery dates are confirmed for each product only after the specification has been reviewed.

Embroidered headboards

Complete upholstered headboards, or embroidered panels for your upholsterer. Repeat production for hotel room schedules; no fixed minimum. Production typically takes 3–4 months; more complex projects may require up to 6 months.

Embroidered wall panels

Bespoke panels for suites, corridors and public areas, from a single piece to a full wall, supplied for installation by your appointed contractor. No fixed minimum. Production typically takes 3–4 months; more complex projects may require up to 6 months.

Embroidered fabric

Furnishing fabric by the metre for upholstery and curtains. Typical lead time 6–8 weeks; complex work up to 6 months.

How a hospitality project runs

From schedule to dispatch.

01UnderstandThe rug schedule — rooms, quantities, sizes — with drawings, scheme and application. We come back with our thoughts, questions and what still needs resolving.
02DevelopConstruction, yarn, pile and colour worked through for each rug type. Where there is enough information, an initial cost range follows.
03SampleYarn samples, colour trials and woven strike-offs, approved in the hand before production. A strike-off takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved, and its cost is credited against the order — plan it against any mock-up room date.
04MakeWeaving, tufting or flatweaving to each size, in the workshop suited to the construction, with progress photography at agreed points.
05CheckQuality control against the specification and approved sample — dimensions, pile, colour, binding, backing and finishing — reviewed in Delhi.
06DeliverRugs are usually shipped rolled and wrapped, with export documentation and freight coordinated through our logistics partners. Prices are usually quoted ex-works in USD or EUR; FOB or DDP on request.

For embroidery, strike-offs, where required, typically take 2–3 weeks. Delivery dates are confirmed only after the specification has been reviewed. Installation on site is by the project’s appointed contractor.

Specification and documentation

Agreed before the order.

Project-specific documentation and testing requirements can be discussed during specification. Any required standards and supporting evidence must be assessed and agreed before the order is accepted. We do not hold product certifications, and the ability to arrange testing for a project is not a guarantee of a result. Please include any fire, wear or cleaning requirements in the brief.

We say plainly where a material or construction is unsuited to the traffic a room will take.

What to send

A useful first brief.

  • Rug schedule — room or area, quantity and size for each rug type
  • Embroidery — headboards, wall panels or fabric for the same rooms, with quantities by room type
  • Design intent — artwork, scheme, references, or the collection designs you are considering
  • Construction preference — or leave it to our recommendation
  • Specification requirements — any performance, cleaning or documentation standards
  • Programme — mock-up room date, target delivery date and project location
Contracting

One supplier, one point of contact.

Artilier is the trade studio of Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Orders are contracted, invoiced and exported through that entity, with established export documentation, and you have one point of contact from brief to dispatch. Costing and lead times are given per project once construction, materials, sizes and sampling are understood.
