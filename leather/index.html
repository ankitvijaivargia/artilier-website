Artilier/Leather
Leather · Cut, laid and stitched

Hide and leather,
made for the wall.

Hair-on cowhide and leather, cut by CNC and by hand, colour-matched, laid and stitched in the atelier. Artilier develops, makes and supplies hide wall panels, leather-clad panels, headboards, wall pieces and trims for interior designers and architects, from the designer’s own drawing or ours. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.

Wall to wall

A panel,
or the whole wall.

Hide panels can be joined to cover a whole wall. Send the elevations with doors, windows and switches marked. We plan the panel layout and the position of the joins with you so the pattern reads across the wall. Installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor.

Leather-clad panels

Leather on
board.

Leather is applied to engineered wood, MDF or plywood, to the client’s specification. Send the board type, sizes, edge details and how the panels will be fixed, and we confirm what can be made. Panels are supplied ready for your installer; installation is carried out at your end.

From drawing to dispatch

Your drawing,
in hide.

Send artwork, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes. We resolve pattern, hide or leather, colour and backing with you before anything is costed in detail.

01BriefDrawing, elevation or reference; dimensions; hide or leather; colour; panel, board or headboard; delivery country; who will install.
02DevelopmentPattern, cutting, colour-matching and backing are worked out against the drawing in our New Delhi studio.
03Strike-offA strike-off of a representative section is made for your approval before production.
04ProductionProduction typically takes 2–4 months, depending on the design and specification. Complex designs may take up to 6 months. We confirm a delivery date only after reviewing the specification.
05DispatchPacked to suit the construction and dispatched as agreed in the quote. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.
Quantity and specification

One panel
or a scheme.

No fixed minimum order quantity. Each project is assessed according to its design, technical requirements and production specifications.

Tanning details and testing can be arranged for a specific project. We do not hold product certifications. Where a project requires testing or documentation, we will tell you before the order is accepted whether it can be arranged for that product and within that timeline.

Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

What hide do you use?
Hair-on cowhide, alongside smooth and metallic leathers.
Do you install the panels?
No. We make and supply the panels; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor.
Can you cover a whole wall?
Yes. Hide panels are joined to cover the wall. Mark doors, windows and switches on the elevations; the layout and joins are agreed with you before production.
How wide can a panel be?
Up to 54 in (approximately 137 cm). Where a wider panel is needed, two widths can be stitched together.
Can you cut our own pattern?
Yes. Send the drawing or CAD file. Geometric patterns such as chevron and hexagon are cut by CNC and by hand.
How long does it take?
Production typically takes 2–4 months, depending on the design and specification. Complex designs may take up to 6 months.
Can leather be paired with a rug or embroidery?
Yes. Rugs, embroidery and leather are developed in the same studio, so colour and pattern can be carried across one room.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs and embroidery.
