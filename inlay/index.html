Inlay furniture · Mother-of-pearl and bone

Mother-of-pearl and bone
inlay, made to the drawing.

For designers and architects who have drawn a piece in shell or bone and need it made properly. ARTILIER develops and makes inlay furniture from your drawings — tables, consoles, cabinets, chests and bedside tables — in our own workshops, with a sample approved before production.

Mother-of-pearl and bone inlayFrom your drawing or oursNo fixed minimum
01 — Pattern

Drawn, then laid
piece by piece.

Geometric, floral or figurative; a pattern from our archive or one drawn by your studio. We redraw it to the piece, set the scale of each element to the size of the furniture, and lay it by hand in shell or bone.

02 — Form

Inlaid on
every face.

Serpentine fronts, drums, pedestals and faceted forms. The pattern is carried round corners and curves, so the piece reads as one surface from every side.

03 — Your specification

What your
studio decides.

Each of these is set at design stage and shown on the sample before production.

Pattern and scaleYour drawing or ours, scaled to the piece
Shell or boneMother-of-pearl for lustre; bone for a flatter, warmer white
Ground colourAny resin colour, matched to your scheme
Form and dimensionsCoffee, side, console and dining tables; cabinets, chests, dressers and bedside tables
Base and metalInlaid plinth, legs, or a brass or metal base and stand
QuantityOne piece, or a matched run across a room schedule
04 — On the workshop floor

Seen from every side.

Finished pieces from our own production, walked round before packing.

Materials

Shell, bone
and resin.

Mother-of-pearl or bone is cut by hand and laid piece by piece into a coloured resin ground over the carcass, then sanded and finished. The pieces shown are built on an MDF carcass; the carcass, ground colour and finish for your piece are set out in the specification. Shell varies in lustre and bone in tone, so the sample shows how the chosen material reads before production.

Residences and hospitality

One piece, or
a room schedule.

A single console for a residence, or bedside tables and chests repeated across guest rooms. For a schedule, send quantities by room type; one approved sample sets the pattern, ground and finish for the run.

From drawing to dispatch

Your drawing,
in inlay.

Send a drawing, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes. We resolve the pattern, material, ground and base with you before anything is costed in detail.

Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

Do you make mother-of-pearl inlay furniture?
Yes. Tables, consoles, chests, bedside tables and dressers, inlaid with mother-of-pearl or bone, made in our own workshops.
Can you make a piece to our drawing?
Yes. Send the drawing or CAD file with the dimensions. The pattern, ground and construction are resolved with you before the sample.
Mother-of-pearl or bone?
Both. Mother-of-pearl carries more lustre; bone reads flatter and warmer. The sample shows how each looks against your ground colour.
Is there a sample?
Yes. A sample is made for your approval before production.
How long does it take?
Production usually takes 6–8 weeks, depending on the design and specification.
Is there a minimum order?
No fixed minimum. One console or a full room schedule.
Do you install?
No. We make and supply. Placement and any fixing on site are by your appointed installer or contractor.
Do you also inlay stone?
Yes. Semi-precious stone and shell inlay for table tops, panels and joinery is on our stone page.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs, embroidery, leather and stone.
