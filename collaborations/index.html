Co-authoredTrade onlyNew Delhi
What collaboration means here

Not a commission.
A co-authorship.

A rug can be chosen from a collection, or commissioned to a brief. A collaboration is a third thing. It starts from someone else's visual language — an artwork, a pattern, a way of using colour, a body of work — and asks what that language becomes when it is made in wool and silk at floor scale. You bring the idea. We bring the reading of it: which construction carries it, what survives the translation, and what has to change to work as a rug. If you need a specific piece for a specific project, on a project timeline, that is bespoke development — the faster and usually the right route. Collaboration is for work meant to exist in its own right.

From idea to rug

Concept to
finished piece.

Four stages, and only the ones we can genuinely stand behind. How long each takes depends on the construction and on how resolved the idea is when it arrives.

01The ideaAn artwork, sketch, pattern, moodboard, colour palette or spatial requirement — or a direction that has not been drawn yet.
02DevelopmentThe idea is read for textile: which construction carries it, how the colour separates, what holds at floor scale and what needs to be redrawn.
03RefinementStrike-offs and colour trials. Density, detail and tone resolved in hand, against the sample, until the piece reads as it was meant to.
04RealisationWoven to final dimensions across our production network in India, checked against the approved sample, finished and packed in Delhi.

Starting points

You do not need
a finished drawing.

Collaborators arrive at different stages and all of them are workable. What matters is that there is a point of view to work from.

A finished artwork

A painting, print, drawing or photograph. We look at how it translates — and tell you honestly where it will resist becoming a rug.

Translation
A sketch or direction

An unresolved idea, a moodboard, a palette, a reference. The drawing gets developed as part of the work rather than before it.

Development
A brief, only

No artwork yet — a space, a use, a feeling, a constraint. The design is developed with you from the brief itself.

From scratch
An existing design

A piece from the collection re-scaled, re-coloured or re-cut toward a different intent.

Adaptation
Credit & confidentiality

Agreed before,
not after.

How the work is credited, what stays confidential and where the design sits are part of the opening conversation, not an afterthought. What is appropriate depends on the collaboration — an artist's edition and a studio's in-house pattern are not the same arrangement — so we settle it with you at the start rather than apply a standard. Contracting, invoicing and export run through Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi; the commercial detail is set out under trade.
