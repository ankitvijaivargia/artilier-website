ARTILIER/Process
Process

From drawing
to delivery.

Six stages, adapted to the piece. Some commissions move quickly; others need more development. We keep the process transparent, with approvals and progress updates at the stages that matter.

Six stagesApprovals throughoutReviewed in Delhi
01UnderstandArtwork, dimensions, colour scheme and application. We read it properly, then come back with our thoughts, our questions and what still needs resolving.
02DevelopConstruction or technique, materials, colour and scale worked through with the workshop suited to the piece. Where there is enough information, an initial cost range follows.
03SampleMaterial samples, colour trials and a strike-off or sample — approved in the hand before full production begins.
04MakeWeaving, embroidery, cutting or inlay to your dimensions, in the workshop suited to the work. Progress photography at agreed points.
05CheckQuality control against the specification and approved sample — dimensions, colour, construction and finishing, reviewed in Delhi.
06DeliverRolling, crating, export documentation and freight coordinated through our logistics partners. Installation is carried out by your appointed contractor; we remain available to answer questions until the piece is in place.
Costing and timing

Precise when
it can be.

Delivery dates are confirmed once we have reviewed the specification. There is no fixed minimum order. Costing becomes more precise as the artwork, construction, materials and sampling requirements are understood. Hand-made production can vary with construction, yarn availability and loom capacity — we communicate changes as they arise rather than after the fact.

RugsUsually 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval; up to 6 months in some cases
Embroidered wall panels & headboards3–4 months; up to 6 months for complex projects
Embroidered fabric6–8 weeks; up to 6 months for complex work
Leather & hide2–4 months; up to 6 months for complex designs
Stone & inlay6–8 weeks after sample approval
Wall works4–12 weeks from sample approval, depending on the material
Strike-offFor rugs, 2–3 weeks once the design is approved, its cost credited against the order
Where the work sits

Studio in Delhi,
made across India.

Studio, artwork development, sampling, finishing and quality control sit in New Delhi. Making runs through established workshops across India, selected for what each piece needs — more on that in the craft. Before you brief us, the contact page links a briefing guide for each discipline.
