ARTILIER/Wall works
Wall works · Relief, sculpture and fibre

Wall pieces,
made to the drawing.

Sculptural wall pieces and relief panels in ceramic, metal, plaster, carved and assembled wood, and natural fibre. ARTILIER develops, makes and supplies them for designers, architects and galleries, from the designer’s own drawing or ours, sized to the room and finished for where they will hang. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.

The work

Five materials,
one studio.

Each material is worked by a specialist workshop and brought through one approval process in our New Delhi studio. You deal with one team, one drawing set and one quotation, whether the brief is a single piece or a scheme across several rooms.

Ceramic and porcelainHand-built relief, coral and wave forms, clustered plates and tiles; glazed, matt or raw
MetalRolled, grooved and etched sections assembled into wall sculpture; toned or patinated
Plaster and cast reliefTexture paintings, framed relief panels and hand-cast surfaces, from one frame to a full wall
Carved and assembled woodCarved relief and three-dimensional block assemblies, stained or washed to a colour reference
Natural fibreRattan, raffia, hemp and hand-wrapped yarn; fringed, woven, coiled or framed
Repeat piecesOne design made to the same standard across a hotel’s rooms or a residence’s suites
In the making

Surface by surface.

Details of work from our workshops, shown in part. Complete pieces, with sizes and finishes, are shared with design studios on request.

Where they hang

Finished for
the room.

Tell us where each piece will go: a living room, a corridor, a bathroom or a covered terrace. Glazes, sealants, finishes and the fixing method are chosen for the location and agreed at drawing stage, before the sample is made.

Hospitality and residences

One piece, or
a room schedule.

A single commission for a villa, or the same piece repeated across a hotel’s keys with variations by room type. Send the schedule with sizes and quantities and we quote each line against the drawing. Hospitality & contract →

Size

Sized at
design stage.

The largest size depends on the material and the piece. We confirm what can be made in one piece, and where a large wall is divided into sections and where the joins fall, once we have seen the drawing.

From drawing to dispatch

Your drawing,
on the wall.

Send a sketch, artwork, a CAD elevation or a reference with the sizes. We resolve material, form, colour and finish with you before anything is costed in detail.

01BriefDrawing or reference; dimensions; material; where it will hang; quantity; delivery country; who will install.
02DevelopmentForm, material, colour and finish are worked out against the drawing in our New Delhi studio.
03SampleA sample in the chosen material and finish is made for your approval before production.
04ProductionProduction usually takes 4–12 weeks from sample approval, depending on the material, size and quantity. We confirm a delivery date after reviewing the specification.
05DispatchPacked to suit the material and dispatched as agreed in the quote. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.
Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

Which materials do you work in?
Ceramic and porcelain, metal, plaster and cast relief, carved and assembled wood, and natural fibre: rattan, raffia, hemp and wrapped yarn. Ask if you have another material in mind.
Can you make our own design?
Yes. Send the drawing, artwork or CAD elevation. Material, form and finish are resolved with you before the sample.
Can you make the same piece for every room?
Yes. Repeat pieces for hotel rooms and suites are made to the approved sample, with sizes and quantities taken from your schedule.
Can pieces go in bathrooms or outdoors?
Bathrooms and covered outdoor spaces, yes, with the material and finish chosen for the location. Fully exposed sites are reviewed per project.
How large can a piece be?
It depends on the material and the piece. We confirm the size, and any sections or joins, at design stage.
Is there a sample?
Yes. A sample is made for your approval before production.
How long does it take?
Production usually takes 4–12 weeks from sample approval, depending on the material, size and quantity.
Do you install?
No. We make and supply; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor. The fixing method is agreed with you at drawing stage.
Is there a minimum order?
No fixed minimum. One piece or a full room schedule.
Can we see complete pieces?
Yes. We share a reference set of complete pieces, with sizes and finishes, with design studios on request.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs, embroidery, leather and stone.
