Privacy

Privacynotice.

This notice explains what information Artilier collects through artilier.co and in the course of its trade enquiries, why, how long it is kept and the choices you have. Artilier is a studio of Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi, India, which is responsible for this information. Write to studio@artilier.co about anything in this notice.

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Enquiries

What you send us.

When you use an enquiry form we receive what you enter — your name, studio or company, email address, where you are based, the type of project, construction, a description of the project, quantities, dimensions, target date and project location. With it we receive the page you sent it from, the page you first arrived on, the website that referred you, any campaign tags in the link you followed and, where relevant, the design or product page that led you to the form.

The form is delivered to our studio inbox by Formspree, a form-handling service; we have switched off Formspree’s storage of submissions in its dashboard, so the email in our inbox is our record. If you email us directly or send drawings or artwork, they are held in our studio email, which runs on Google Workspace. If you message us on WhatsApp, the conversation is carried by WhatsApp (part of Meta) under its own terms, and we treat it like email: we use it only to reply to you and about the work you ask about.

We use these details to reply to you, to discuss, quote for and carry out the work you ask about, and to keep a record of our business correspondence. We do not sell them and do not use them for advertising.

Business contacts

Studios we write to.

We introduce our work to interior design and architecture studios. For that we keep business contact details that studios publish themselves — studio name, website, the names and roles of people listed on it, published business email addresses and the projects they show — together with a record of what we sent and any reply. We write only about trade work relevant to the studio. Every email says how to ask us not to write again; if you ask, we stop and keep only what we need to make sure we do not contact you again.

Analytics and cookies

How the site is measured.

Hosting. The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without identifying you. Fonts are served from artilier.co itself.

Google Analytics 4 helps us see which pages are useful and which lead to enquiries. When it runs it sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_8TXE25YJ86, which hold a random identifier and, according to Google, last up to two years. Google states that Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We do not use it for advertising: Google signals, advertising storage and ad personalisation are switched off, and the events we send never include your name, email, phone number or message. In our Google Analytics settings, event-level data is kept for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months.

Your choice. If you visit from the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland — or if we cannot tell where you are — Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookie is set unless you choose Allow analytics. To decide this, the site asks Cloudflare which country your connection comes from; that answer is not stored. Elsewhere, Google Analytics loads by default. Wherever you are, you can allow or withdraw analytics at any time in Cookie settings; withdrawing stops Google Analytics on this site and removes its cookies from your browser.

In your browser. Your cookie choice is remembered in your browser's local storage (artilier_consent) until you change it or clear your browser data. For the length of a visit the site may keep the page you arrived on, the referring site and any campaign tags in session storage (artilier_touch), so that an enquiry you send tells us how you found us; in the UK, EEA and Switzerland this happens only after you allow analytics. Session storage is cleared when you close the tab.

Retention

How long we keep it.

We keep enquiries, related correspondence and business-contact records for 24 months after our last meaningful interaction with you, and then delete them — unless we need to keep them longer because of a contract with you, a legal or accounting obligation, or a dispute, in which case we keep them only for as long as that requires. Where you have asked us not to contact you again, we keep the minimum needed to respect that request.

Where it is processed

Your rights and choices.

We are based in India. Our service providers — Google, Formspree and Cloudflare, and WhatsApp if you message us there — may process information in other countries, including the United States.

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Write to studio@artilier.co; we aim to reply within one month. If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you can also complain to your data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

We will update this notice when our practices change and show the date of the latest version at the top.
