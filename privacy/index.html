Artilier/Privacy
Privacy

Privacynotice.

This notice explains what information artilier.co collects, why, and the choices you have. Artilier is a trade studio of Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi, India, which is responsible for this information. Contact: studio@artilier.co.

Enquiries

What you send us.

When you use an enquiry form we receive what you type — name, studio, email, location and project details — together with the page you sent it from, the page you first arrived on, the referring website and any campaign tags in the link. The form is delivered to our studio inbox by Formspree, a form service. We use these details only to reply to you and to handle the project you ask about.

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and to keep business records. [Owner to confirm the retention period before publication.]

Analytics

How the site is measured.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

Google Analytics 4 helps us see which pages are useful and which lead to enquiries. It sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_8TXE25YJ86, which hold a random identifier; Google states that it does not store IP addresses in Google Analytics 4. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, and Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off. Analytics events never include your name, email, phone number or message.

If you visit from the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, Google Analytics does not load and no analytics cookie is set unless you choose Allow analytics. Elsewhere it loads by default. You can change your choice at any time through Cookie settings.

For the length of a visit, the site may keep the page you arrived on and the referring site in your browser's session storage, so that an enquiry you send tells us how you found us. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland this happens only after you allow analytics. Your cookie choice itself is remembered in your browser's local storage.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion.

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to studio@artilier.co. Our service providers, including Google and Formspree, may process information outside India, including in the United States. If you are in the UK or the EEA you can also complain to your data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.
