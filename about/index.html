About the studio

A design studio
in New Delhi.

Artilier is a design-led studio in New Delhi bringing together interior design thinking, material understanding and hands-on manufacturing experience. We work trade only, with interior designers, architects and design practices internationally. Our first collection is rugs.

How the studio is built

Artilier is new.
The experience behind it isn't.

That combination is what closes the gap between a beautiful drawing and a rug that can actually be made well — where artwork, construction, yarn, colour, proportion, cost and finishing all have to come together.

The people

Two perspectives. One studio.

Every artwork, drawing and specification passes through both: one person who has spent over a decade making things, and one who designs the interiors those things go into.

Ankit Vijaivargia

Founder · Manufacturing & Development. Ankit brings over a decade of experience in custom manufacturing from New Delhi, with deep knowledge of materials, construction and production. He works on what a design will take to make — yarn, construction, knot count, pile, sampling, costing and quality control — and stays on it from the first drawing to the finished rug.

Payal Samal

Design Head · Interior Designer. Payal is an interior designer by profession and brings a spatial, design-led perspective — colour, material, proportion, pattern and how pieces live within interiors. She leads the studio's design direction: the collection, colourways and the development of bespoke artwork.

What we do

Rugs, developed and made.

  • Bespoke development — rugs developed from a designer’s artwork, drawing, CAD file or colour scheme. Bespoke rugs
  • The collection — named designs in hand-knotted, hand-tufted and flatweave, each a starting point re-coloured and re-sized to the project. The collection
  • Collaborations — rugs co-developed with designers, architects and artists. Collaborations
  • Hospitality and multi-rug projects — schedules of rugs for boutique hospitality and residential work. Hospitality
Artilier and Art Avenue

One supplier, one point of coordination.

Artilier is the trade studio of Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Artilier is where design, development, sampling and the client relationship sit; orders are contracted, invoiced and exported through Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd. — so a project has one supplier, one point of coordination and established export documentation.

Where the work sits

Studio in Delhi, looms across India.

Studio, artwork development, sampling, finishing and quality control sit in New Delhi. Weaving runs through established rug workshops across India, selected for the construction each rug needs. The workshop is chosen to suit the rug; the specification is held and checked in one place. More in the craft and the process.
