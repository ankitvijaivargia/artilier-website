Bespoke rug development

Our development.

Send artwork, a painting, a CAD file, a colour scheme, a reference image or a floor plan, and we develop it into a finished rug — construction, yarn, colour, scale and finish worked out with you, then proved in a sample before production.

How it works

Three stages,
one conversation.

Custom dimensions are not an add-on here — every rug is made to order. Custom colourways are developed against your scheme, and samples are available for approval before production.

01Your artworkPainting, drawing, CAD, scheme or reference. Whatever exists — including a rough idea and a floor plan.
02Our developmentConstruction, yarn, knot count, pile, colour separation and scale. Strike-offs and colour trials until it reads right.
03Your rugWoven to your dimensions, checked against the approved sample, finished and packed in Delhi.

Construction

Which construction
a design wants.

Which construction a design wants is a technical decision, not a style one. We make the recommendation from the artwork, the detail it carries, the traffic it will take and the budget — and prove it in sampling before production. Existing designs in the collection can equally be re-scaled and re-coloured to a scheme rather than developed from scratch.

Sampling and timing

Proved in a sample,
then made.

Development runs in a fixed order, so you always know what is being decided and when.

Yarn samples Approved in hand
Colour trials Against your scheme
Woven strike-off Sized to the design · 2–3 weeks · credited against the order
Initial cost range Once there is enough information
Lead time Usually 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval
Minimum order None — single pieces welcome
Progress photography At agreed points
Pricing USD or EUR · usually ex-works

Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval and can extend to six months in some cases; we confirm the timeline for each project, and costing becomes more precise as the artwork, construction, materials and sampling are resolved. Hand-made production varies with construction, yarn availability and loom capacity — hand-knotting takes longest, hand-tufting is shorter — and we tell you about changes as they arise, not after the fact. Before you write, our guide to briefing a bespoke rug sets out what to send.
