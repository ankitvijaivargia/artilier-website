ARTILIER/The craft
The craft

Made by hand,
and it shows.

ARTILIER is a design-led studio in New Delhi bringing together interior design thinking, material understanding and hands-on manufacturing experience. We work across six disciplines — rugs, hand embroidery, leather, stone, inlay furniture and wall works — from workshops across India.

New DelhiMade across IndiaMade to order
01 — Rug weaving

Looms across
India.

Weaving runs through established rug workshops across India, selected for the construction each rug needs rather than for convenience. Construction, yarn, knot count, pile height, colour separation and scale are worked through with the weaving workshop, and yarn, spin and dye lot are approved in the hand before a loom is set. Rug materials.

01Hand-knottedKnot by knot on the loom. Traditional hand-knotting for high-detail, high-density rugs: the finest definition, the longest lead time, and the construction that carries painterly artwork best. See the designs.
02Hand-tuftedTufted to the drawing. A more flexible construction for certain designs, textures and project requirements — carving, mixed pile heights and loop-and-cut surfaces, at a shorter lead time. See the designs.
03FlatweaveLow profile, woven flat. Suited to specific applications and design requirements — lighter, reversible in some constructions, and a different surface underfoot. See the designs.

Which construction a design wants is a technical decision, not a style one. We make the recommendation from the artwork, the detail it carries, the traffic it will take and the budget — and prove it in sampling before production.

02 — Embroidery

Worked on
the frame.

Zardozi, zari, aari, raffia and hand embroidery. Each piece is worked on the frame in our New Delhi studio and in the established workshops we work with across India, then checked in Delhi before it leaves — for wall panels, framed art, headboards, upholstery panels and drapery. Embroidery at ARTILIER.

03 — Stone, inlay & mother-of-pearl

The master
atelier.

Semi-precious stone, marble and shell are cut piece by piece and inlaid in the atelier, with mother-of-pearl and abalone set alongside stone. Inlay furniture is made in our own workshops: mother-of-pearl or bone cut by hand and laid piece by piece into a coloured resin ground over the carcass, then sanded and finished. Stone · Inlay furniture.

04 — New Delhi · Studio, sampling & quality

Developed and
checked in Delhi.

Studio, artwork development, sampling, finishing and quality control sit in New Delhi. Leather and wall works are developed here too: pattern, cutting and colour-matching for leather, and form, material and finish for wall works in ceramic, metal, plaster, wood and natural fibre, are worked out against the drawing in our New Delhi studio. Each piece is checked against the specification and the approved sample, then finished and packed in Delhi. Leather · Wall works · The process.

Beyond

Built around making,
not one product.

ARTILIER is built around art, material and making rather than around a single product. Rugs, hand embroidery, leather, stone, inlay furniture and wall works are where the studio works today; design objects follow. The constant is the model: made to order by hand, developed from Delhi, sold trade only. How we work with practices.
