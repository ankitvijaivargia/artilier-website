# ARTILIER — international SEO strategy

Date: 10 Oct 2026 · Markets: UAE, Saudi Arabia, France (priority 1); UK, US (priority 2)

## Ground rules

- One studio, in New Delhi, stated plainly on every market page. No local offices, addresses, phone numbers or "showrooms" implied.
- One market page per country — never per city. Cities are named where the studio genuinely works with designers there.
- `hreflang` only between pages that are true language equivalents and are maintained together. Today there are none (the French page has no English equivalent), so **no hreflang is set**; the French page carries `lang="fr"`, `og:locale fr_FR`, and links to English pages are marked `hreflang="en"`.
- Market targeting is done with content (terminology, time zones, working weeks, units, programme realities), internal links and earned local signals — not with geo-tagged doorway pages.
- Search Console: once verified, do **not** set an international target country (the site serves several).

## 6. Internal linking requirements

- Footer links every market page and the French page sitewide (in place).
- Market pages link to each other, to hospitality and to the briefing guide (branch).
- Case studies, when they exist, link to their country's market page and the market page links back.
- The French page links to English pages with `hreflang="en"` on the link until French equivalents exist.

## 7. Expansion beyond these markets

Only with evidence: Search Console impressions from a new country for non-branded rug queries over two consecutive months, plus at least one qualified enquiry from that country. Qatar (Design Doha) and Kuwait are the most likely next Gulf candidates; Italy and Germany are not recommended without data.
