01 — What we do

One piece,
or a collection.

Artilier makes rugs, hand embroidery, leather and stone to commission. Some briefs are a single piece for one room; others are a collection, developed as a set and made to the same standard from the first piece to the last.

10 — Philosophy

Precision,
with character.

A hand-made piece is made by people, not machines. We work carefully to the agreed artwork, dimensions, materials and finish — and at the same time, natural materials and human hands leave their own trace. Small differences in weave, stitch, hide and stone are part of what makes a hand-made rug individual.

We don't try to remove that character. We try to make sure it is intentional and considered.

What is checked

  • Dimensions against the approved artwork
  • Materials approved before production
  • Finishes reviewed against the sample
  • Samples or strike-offs before production
  • Workmanship inspected before dispatch

What is character

  • Natural variation in yarn, thread and hide
  • Marking and colour that differ stone to stone
  • Subtle differences in hand-finishing
  • Slight differences in dye lot and abrash
  • Individual marks left by the making

Handmade does not mean careless. A defect is a defect — it is not dismissed as character.

11 — Process

From drawing
to delivery.

Six stages, adapted to the piece. Some commissions move quickly; others need more development. We keep the process transparent, with approvals and progress updates at the stages that matter.

01UnderstandArtwork, dimensions, colour scheme and application. We read it properly, then come back with our thoughts, our questions and what still needs resolving.
02DevelopConstruction or technique, materials, colour and scale worked through with the workshop suited to the piece. Where there is enough information, an initial cost range follows.
03SampleMaterial samples, colour trials and a strike-off or sample — approved in the hand before full production begins.
04MakeWeaving, embroidery, cutting or inlay to your dimensions, in the workshop suited to the work. Progress photography at agreed points.
05CheckQuality control against the specification and approved sample — dimensions, colour, construction and finishing, reviewed in Delhi.
06DeliverRolling, crating, export documentation and freight coordinated through our logistics partners. Installation is carried out by your appointed contractor; we remain available to answer questions until the piece is in place.
