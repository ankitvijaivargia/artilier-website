# ARTILIER — competitor and keyword research

Date: 10 Oct 2026

## Method and limits — read first

- **No measured keyword data.** Semrush had no API units and Ahrefs returned "Insufficient plan" for every endpoint, including the free Domain Rating lookup. This document therefore contains **no search volumes, difficulty scores, rankings, backlink counts, authority scores or traffic estimates**. Where demand is discussed, it is inferred from what the results contain, and labelled as such.
- **Results are observed, not Google positions.** Research used WebSearch (a US-based index) and WebFetch on 40+ competitor pages. It does not reproduce Google.ae, Google.com.sa, Google.fr or Google.co.uk, and result order is the order the tool returned. Before committing significant effort to a query, check it from the target country (VPN or a rank tracker).
- Full working notes with every URL are kept outside the repo; the findings below are the decision-relevant extract.

Labels: **Observed** (seen in results/pages) · **Inference** (reasoned from observations) · **Recommendation**.

## 1. Cross-market findings

1. **Residential bespoke rugs and contract hotel carpet are different markets.** (Observed) Residential queries are won by showrooms, dealers and design press; hotel/contract queries by broadloom manufacturers (Brintons, Ulster, Wilton, Tai Ping, Balsan) through named project case studies and trade-press write-ups. ARTILIER fits residential and the **hand-made feature-rug layer** of hospitality (lobbies, suites, F&B, ballroom and majlis rugs, branded residences) — not wall-to-wall Axminster. (Inference)
2. **Trade-programme pages are thin everywhere.** (Observed) The Rug Company, Ramsha, Samad, Nazmiyal and Tai Ping link to trade programmes but publish few terms. Only The Rug Company (bespoke 22+ weeks), Margo Selby (16–22 weeks by construction), Toulemonde Bochart (12–14 weeks) and a German dealer (30×30 cm strike-off, fee refunded) publish concrete process facts. ARTILIER already publishes more working terms than most competitors. (Inference)
3. **Nobody answers the specifier's technical questions.** (Observed) No ranking page explains what a rug strike-off is with real sizes, costs and approval steps; no informational page ranks for "custom rug from CAD drawing"; "hotel rug specification fire rating" has no area-rug answer. This is the clearest content opening. (Inference)
4. **Case studies win hospitality.** (Observed) Brintons and Ulster rank for Saudi and UK hotel queries with named-project pages even without a local office. ARTILIER has no published projects. (Gap)
5. **Remote suppliers can rank for Gulf city queries honestly.** (Observed) For "custom rugs Jeddah", results included pages from a US dealer and a German retailer that do not claim a local office.
6. **US and UK city queries are local-showroom SERPs.** (Observed) Dallas, Houston and "bespoke rugs London" results favour addresses, showrooms, BIID/Houzz profiles and local press. An overseas studio should not imitate local presence. (Recommendation)

### 2.5 United States (Priority 2)

Three distinct buying contexts (observed): (1) designers commissioning custom residential rugs — NY dealers' trade programmes and guide hubs (Doris Leslie Blau, Nazmiyal); (2) designers sourcing through showrooms — D&D Building, Dallas Design District, Decorative Center Houston, with memo pulls and holds; (3) hospitality procurement — Nourison, Tai Ping, Ulster, with project pages and contract catalogues. ARTILIER can honestly serve (1), and the feature-rug part of (3) once projects exist. **Dallas and Houston queries are local-showroom SERPs**; do not build city pages that imply presence.

Notable: **Doris Leslie Blau is publishing a large cluster of near-duplicate, thin articles** on specifying and sampling custom rugs (observed, many ~300 words). Quality, not volume, is the way to compete.
