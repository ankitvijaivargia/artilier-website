ARTILIER/Briefing a wall works commission
Guide · For designers and architects

How to brief
a wall works commission.

What to send, what gets decided and in what order, and how the studio’s own stages run — for wall pieces and relief panels in ceramic, metal, plaster, wood and natural fibre. Written from the way our New Delhi studio runs commissions for interior designers, architects and galleries.

Specifier guideMedium · scale · relief · timing
Before you write

Four things decide the piece.

  • 1. Medium — What the piece is made from. Each has different weight, texture, finish, and environmental requirements:
  • Ceramic and porcelain — Glazed or unglazed. Suitable for wet areas and high-traffic walls. Custom glaze colours.
  • Metal — Brass, copper, steel, aluminium. Forged, welded, or cast. Patinated, polished, brushed, or lacquered. Structural load is a consideration.
  • Plaster and cast relief — Gypsum or lime. Carved, moulded, or cast. Lightweight. Can be painted, gilded, or left raw.
  • Carved and assembled wood — Teak, sheesham, mango wood, reclaimed timber. CNC-routed or hand-carved.
  • Natural fibre — Rattan, raffia, hemp, hand-wrapped yarn. Woven, coiled, or assembled. Protected interior environments only.
  • 2. Scale and dimensions — Total wall area or panel dimensions. Number of panels and join method (seamless, revealed, modular). Relief depth: low (10–20mm), medium, or high/sculptural (50mm+). Weight per panel and wall substrate load capacity.
  • 3. Design detail — Pattern (geometric, organic, figurative, abstract, or client artwork). Surface finish. Colour. Texture and how it interacts with lighting — raking light, downlighting, and daylight all reveal relief differently.
  • 4. Programme — Lead time varies by medium:
  • Ceramic/porcelain: 8–12 weeks
  • Metal: 6–10 weeks
  • Plaster/cast relief: 4–8 weeks
  • Carved wood: 6–10 weeks
  • Natural fibre: 4–8 weeks
  • Maquette/sample: 2–3 weeks (credited against order)
The design

Send what exists, at whatever stage.

  • Architectural drawings or CAD (elevations, sections showing depth allowance)
  • Reference images or moodboard
  • Colour/finish references (Pantone, RAL, physical samples, or photos)
  • Lighting plan if available
  • Structural notes: wall substrate, load capacity, moisture/environmental exposure
  • Mounting preference: adhesive, mechanical fixings, French cleats, or structural integration
  • Project timeline and installation date
Checklist

A brief we can cost.

  • 1. Medium (ceramic, metal, plaster, wood, or fibre)
  • 2. Dimensions and number of panels
  • 3. Relief depth
  • 4. Pattern and design reference
  • 5. Surface finish
  • 6. Wall substrate and load capacity
  • 7. Target date on site
  • 8. Project location
