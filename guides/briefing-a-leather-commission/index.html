Guide · For designers and architects

How to brief
a leather commission.

What to send, what gets decided and in what order, and how the studio’s own stages run — for hide wall panels, leather-clad panels and leather work. Written from the way our New Delhi studio runs commissions for interior designers and architects.

Specifier guideDrawings · hide · panels · timing
Before you write

Four things decide the piece.

A leather commission comes down to four decisions: the piece (hide wall panel, leather-clad panel, headboard, wall piece or trim), the pattern (the cut and layout), the hide or leather and its colour, and the programme (the date it must be on site). The hide or leather, the pattern, the backing and how the piece will be fixed are worked out together once we have seen the design.

The design

Send what exists, at whatever stage.

  • A drawing or CAD file — hide is cut by CNC and by hand to your pattern, or to ours
  • An elevation — for wall schemes, with doors, windows and switches marked
  • A reference — chevron, hexagon and other geometric patterns are common starting points; say what you want to keep
  • Colour — hides are colour-matched; send the references the scheme already uses

Send artwork, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes. Pattern, hide or leather, colour and backing are resolved with you before anything is costed in detail.

The product

Hide, leather, or leather on board.

  • Hide wall panels — hair-on cowhide cut in geometric patterns or to your drawing, laid piece by piece onto a non-woven backing
  • Leather-clad panels — leather applied to engineered wood, MDF or plywood, to your specification. Send the board type, sizes, edge details and how the panels will be fixed
  • Headboards, wall pieces and trims — headboards wrapped in leather or hide; wall pieces in leather and hand-set nails; cut, layered and stitched trims for furniture and door panels

We work in hair-on cowhide, alongside smooth and metallic leathers.

Size

Resolve it from the wall.

  • Panel width — up to 54 in (about 137 cm); two widths can be stitched together where a wider panel is needed
  • Wall to wall — hide panels can be joined to cover a whole wall; layout and joins are agreed with you before production
  • Installation — panels are supplied ready for your installer; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor
Sampling

A strike-off before production.

A strike-off of a representative section is made for your approval before production begins.

Timing and terms

Plan back from the site date.

  • Production — typically 2–4 months, depending on the design and specification; complex designs up to 6 months
  • Delivery date — confirmed only after the specification is reviewed
  • Minimum order — no fixed minimum; one panel or a scheme
  • Dispatch — packed to suit the construction and dispatched as agreed in the quote

Tanning details and testing can be arranged for a specific project. We do not hold product certifications: if the project needs testing or documentation, put it in the brief and we confirm before the order is accepted whether it can be arranged within the timeline.

Checklist

A brief we can cost.

  • Drawing, elevation or reference, with dimensions
  • Hide or leather, and colour
  • Product: hide panel, board-mounted panel, headboard, wall piece or trim
  • For board-mounted work: board type, sizes, edge details and fixing method
  • Quantity: one panel, or a scheme
  • Delivery country and the date on site
  • Who will install
  • Specification requirements: any documentation or testing the project needs
Questions

Asked before the first brief.

Can you cut our own pattern?
Yes. Send the drawing or CAD file with the sizes; pattern, cutting and backing are resolved with you before costing.
Can you cover a whole wall?
Yes. Hide panels are joined to cover the wall. Mark doors, windows and switches on the elevations; layout and joins are agreed with you before production.
Do you install the panels?
No. We make and supply the panels; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor.
Is there a minimum order?
No fixed minimum. Each project is assessed according to its design, technical requirements and production specification.
