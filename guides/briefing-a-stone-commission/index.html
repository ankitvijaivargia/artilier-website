ARTILIER/Briefing a stone commission
Guide · For designers and architects

How to brief
a stone commission.

What to send, what gets decided and in what order, and how the studio’s own stages run — for inlaid table tops, panels, furniture surfaces and objects in stone and shell. Written from the way our New Delhi studio runs commissions for designers, architects and galleries.

Specifier guideDrawings · stones · samples · timing
Before you write

Four things decide the piece.

A stone commission comes down to four decisions: the piece (table top, furniture surface, door or cabinet panel, wall panel, relief piece or object), the stones, the ground they are set in, and the programme. Tell us what the piece is, where it goes and what it sits on; the stones, the ground, the cutting and the size are worked out with you at design stage.

The design

Send what exists, at whatever stage.

  • A drawing or CAD file — your own design is inlaid as drawn
  • A reference — figurative, floral or geometric; say what you want to keep
  • Dimensions — for table tops, the outline and thickness; for architectural work, the elevations
  • Stones, if chosen — otherwise we propose them with the drawing

Send artwork, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes. The stones, ground, cutting and finish are resolved with you before anything is costed in detail.

The stones

What we set.

Malachite, lapis lazuli, rose quartz, crystal quartz, selenite, onyx and marble, with mother-of-pearl and abalone shell. Ask if you have another stone in mind. Every stone varies in colour and marking, and shell varies piece to piece in lustre and colour, which is why a sample shows how the chosen stones read together before production.

The product

Table, panel, or object.

  • Inlaid table tops — dining, coffee and side tables in semi-precious stone inlay or laid stone; stones, ground and any joins agreed at design stage
  • Furniture surfaces — tables, consoles and side pieces in solid or clad stone, to your drawing
  • Door and cabinet panels — stone, mother-of-pearl or abalone laid onto panels, with metal trims where specified
  • Wall panels and relief pieces — inlay on marble, onyx or quartz grounds for walls, doors and joinery, and stone set in relief
  • Objects — trays, boxes and smaller pieces in stone and shell

Installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor. Name them in the brief so fixing and finishing can be agreed before production.

Size

Sized at design stage.

The largest size possible depends on the piece, the stone and the ground. We confirm what can be made in one piece, and where joins fall, once we have seen the drawing. Panels for architectural work are made to the elevations in your drawings.

Sampling

A sample before production.

A sample is made for your approval before production, showing how the chosen stones and shell read together.

Timing and terms

Plan back from the site date.

  • Production — usually 6–8 weeks, depending on the design and specification
  • Delivery date — confirmed after the specification is reviewed
  • Minimum order — no fixed minimum
  • Dispatch — crated to suit the piece and dispatched as agreed in the quote

Add the time to approve the design, the sample, your approval of it, production, and freight to site.

Checklist

A brief we can cost.

  • Drawing, CAD file or reference, with dimensions
  • Piece: table top, furniture, panel, relief or object
  • Stones and ground, or “propose them”
  • Quantity
  • Delivery country and the date on site
  • Who will install
  • Specification requirements: any documentation the project needs
Questions

Asked before the first brief.

Can you inlay our own design?
Yes. Send the drawing or CAD file. The stones and the cutting are resolved with you before the sample.
Do you work in mother-of-pearl?
Yes. Mother-of-pearl and abalone shell are set alongside stone or laid onto panels, from your drawing or ours.
How large can a piece be?
It depends on the piece, the stone and the ground. We confirm the possible size, and any joins, at design stage.
Is there a sample?
Yes. A sample is made for your approval before production.
