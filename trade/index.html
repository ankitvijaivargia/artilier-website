Costing

What we can say,
and when.

There is no minimum order — we make single pieces as readily as multi-rug schedules. A woven strike-off takes 2–3 weeks once the design or sketch is approved, and its cost is credited against the order. Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval, extending to six months in some cases. Costing follows once construction, materials, dimensions and sampling requirements are understood, and becomes more precise as the artwork is resolved. Prices are quoted in USD or EUR, usually ex-works; FOB or DDP can be quoted separately on request. Payment terms are set out in the quote, and rugs are usually shipped rolled. Hand-made production can vary with construction, yarn availability and loom capacity, and we communicate changes as they arise. See the process for where each of those decisions is taken.
