Custom rugs

For interior designers and architects in New York, the Hamptons, Dallas, Houston and Austin: custom rugs developed from your artwork or scheme in our New Delhi studio, approved in a strike-off, and made to any size.

To the tradeFeet or metresStrike-off approved
New York

Manhattan and the Hamptons.

Apartments and townhouses where a rug has to work to an exact furniture plan, and Hamptons houses briefed against the date the house opens for the season. Tell us the date the rug must be on site; sampling and production are planned back from it.

Texas

Dallas, Houston and Austin.

Custom homes and large living spaces where standard sizes rarely fit. Every rug is made to order, so the size follows the room — send the floor plan and we resolve proportions with you before sampling.

Working together

Briefed by email, approved in hand.

New Delhi is nine hours thirty minutes ahead of New York during daylight saving time (ten hours thirty in winter), and Texas is an hour further behind. Work moves by email: a brief sent at the end of your day reaches us at the start of ours. Send sizes in feet and inches or metres, whichever your drawings use. Samples and strike-offs are approved in the hand before production.

Questions

From US practices.

Do you work with private clients?
No — Artilier works to the trade only, with interior designers, architects and design practices.
Can a Hamptons rug be timed to the season?
Tell us the date the rug must be on site. Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks and can extend to six months in some cases, so the earlier the brief, the more options remain open.
Who do we contract with?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., our manufacturing company in New Delhi. Artilier is its trade studio; orders are contracted, invoiced and exported through that entity.
