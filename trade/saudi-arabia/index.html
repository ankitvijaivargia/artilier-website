Trade · Saudi Arabia

Custom rugs for
Riyadh, Jeddah and AlUla.

For interior designers, architects and hospitality teams working in Saudi Arabia: rugs developed from your scheme or artwork in our New Delhi studio, approved in sample, and made to the dimensions of each room.

Saudi projectsResidential & hospitalitySampled for approval
Project types

Residences, hospitality, destinations.

  • Riyadh and Jeddah residences — villas, palaces and apartments, with rugs made to each room rather than to standard sizes
  • Hospitality — hotels and resorts, including destination projects such as AlUla, usually briefed as a schedule of rug types across many rooms. See hospitality
  • Rugs from your own artwork — a pattern, painting or CAD file developed into a finished rug. See bespoke development
Planning

Long programmes, early schedules.

Larger Saudi projects often run on long programmes with several rug types per building. Send the full rug schedule as early as you can: sampling can then be planned against the programme — including any mock-up room — and each rug type approved in the hand before production begins.

On Saudi specifications “carpet” is common, particularly in hospitality. Our work is hand-made rugs in three constructions — hand-knotted, hand-tufted and flatweave — made to each room’s size.

Working together

Two and a half hours apart.

Saudi Arabia (AST, UTC+3) is two hours thirty minutes behind New Delhi (IST, UTC+5:30), and the Saudi working week runs Sunday to Thursday. Briefs, drawings and approvals move by email; samples and strike-offs are approved in the hand before production.

What to send

A useful Saudi brief.

  • Rug schedule — by building or phase: room, quantity and size in metres or centimetres
  • Design intent — artwork, scheme or collection designs under consideration
  • Specification requirements — any performance, cleaning or documentation standards the project sets
  • Programme — mock-up date, delivery city and target handover
Questions

From Saudi practices.

Can sampling be planned around a mock-up room?
Yes. Give us the mock-up date and the rug types it needs; yarn samples, colour trials and strike-offs are scheduled back from it and approved in the hand.
Can one supplier cover several rug types?
Yes. Hand-knotted, hand-tufted and flatweave rugs can sit in one schedule, developed by Artilier and contracted, invoiced and exported through one entity, Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd.
What are the lead times, and is there a minimum?
Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval and can extend to six months in some cases. There is no minimum order — a single rug is welcome. Costing follows once construction, materials, dimensions and sampling are understood.
