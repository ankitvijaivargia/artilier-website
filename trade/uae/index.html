Rugs or carpets

Brief us in the word your drawings use.

On UAE specifications, “bespoke carpet” and “bespoke rug” are often used for the same thing: a hand-made piece made to a room’s size and layout. Our work is hand-made rugs in three constructions — hand-knotted, hand-tufted and flatweave — and we recommend the construction from the artwork, the detail it carries and the traffic the room will take.

Typical briefs

Large rooms, exact sizes.

  • Villas and private residences — living and reception rooms, majlis, bedrooms and dining rooms, each rug made to the room rather than to a standard size
  • Penthouses and apartments — rugs proportioned to furniture layouts and open-plan zones
  • Boutique hospitality — suites, lobbies and restaurants, often as a schedule of several rug types. See hospitality
  • Rugs from your own artwork — a painting, pattern or CAD file developed into a finished rug. See bespoke development
Working together

An hour and a half apart.

New Delhi (IST, UTC+5:30) is one hour thirty minutes ahead of the UAE (GST, UTC+4), and the UAE working week largely runs Monday to Friday — so most of a Dubai or Abu Dhabi working day overlaps with ours. Briefs, drawings and approvals move by email; samples and strike-offs are approved in the hand before production.

What to send

A useful UAE brief.

  • Rug schedule — room, quantity and size, in metres or centimetres
  • Design intent — artwork, scheme or the collection designs you are considering
  • Application — where each rug sits (majlis, bedroom, lobby…) so material and construction suit the traffic
  • Delivery — emirate and city, the site contact or fit-out contractor, and the target handover date
Questions

From UAE practices.

Can we approve samples before production?
Yes. Yarn samples, colour trials and a woven strike-off are approved in the hand before the full rug is set on the loom; the strike-off takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved, and its cost is credited against the order.
What are the lead times, and is there a minimum?
Lead times usually run 6–12 weeks from strike-off approval and can extend to six months in some cases. There is no minimum order — a single rug is welcome. Costing follows once construction, materials, dimensions and sampling are understood.
How are prices quoted, and on what terms?
Prices are quoted in USD or EUR, usually ex-works; FOB or DDP can be quoted separately on request. Payment terms are set out in the quote, and rugs are usually shipped rolled.
Can you make a rug for a majlis or a very large room?
Every rug is made to order, so the size follows the room rather than a standard size. Send the floor plan: proportions are resolved with you before sampling, and any size limit for the chosen construction is confirmed at that stage.
Carpet or rug — does the wording matter?
No. Brief us in the term your drawings use; we confirm the construction, size and finish in the specification.
