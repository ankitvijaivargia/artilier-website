Bespoke, to the trade

Your drawing, developed properly.

We work only with practices — no showroom retail, no consumer pricing. Send a painting, drawing, CAD file or colour scheme; we develop construction, yarn, colour separation and scale with you, then prove it in a woven strike-off before the rug is set on the loom. Collection designs can equally be re-coloured and re-scaled to your scheme.

Typical briefs

Townhouses to boutique hotels.

  • Residential — townhouses, country houses and apartments, with rugs sized to the room and the furniture plan
  • Rugs from artwork — artist or designer collaborations credited to both names. See collaborations
  • Boutique hospitality — suites, public areas and private dining, briefed as a rug schedule. See hospitality
Working together

Four and a half hours ahead.

New Delhi is four hours thirty minutes ahead of London in summer (BST) and five hours thirty minutes in winter (GMT), so a London morning overlaps our afternoon. Briefs and approvals move by email; samples and strike-offs are approved in the hand.

What to send

A useful UK brief.

  • Artwork or scheme — any medium, vector or scan
  • Sizes — in metres, centimetres or feet and inches, or a floor plan
  • Construction — a preference, or leave it to our recommendation
  • Programme — target date on site and delivery address
Questions

From UK practices.

Can the rug be credited to the artist or designer?
Yes. Rugs developed from a designer's or artist's own work can be credited to both names — see collaborations.
Can a collection design be adapted?
Yes. Every design in the collection can be re-scaled, re-proportioned and re-coloured to your scheme and woven to any size.
Can we approve samples before production?
Yes. Yarn samples, colour trials and a woven strike-off are approved in the hand before the full rug is set on the loom; the strike-off takes 2–3 weeks once the design is approved.
