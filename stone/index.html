Artilier/Stone
Stone · Cut, inlaid and set by hand

Stone and shell,
set by hand.

Semi-precious stone, marble and shell, cut piece by piece and inlaid in the atelier. Artilier develops, makes and supplies inlaid panels, table tops, furniture in solid stone, wall pieces and objects for designers, architects and galleries, from the designer’s own drawing or ours. Installation is carried out by the client’s appointed installer or contractor.

Materials

What we
set.

Malachite, lapis lazuli, rose quartz, crystal quartz, selenite, onyx and marble, with mother-of-pearl and abalone shell. Every stone varies in colour and marking; the sample shows how the chosen stones read together before production.

Size

Sized at
design stage.

The largest size possible depends on the piece, the stone and the ground. We confirm what can be made in one piece, and where joins fall, once we have seen the drawing.

From drawing to dispatch

Your drawing,
in stone.

Send artwork, a CAD file or a reference with the sizes. We resolve the stones, ground, cutting and finish with you before anything is costed in detail.

01BriefDrawing or reference; dimensions; stones and ground; panel, top, furniture or object; delivery country; who will install.
02DevelopmentStones, cutting and layout are worked out against the drawing in our New Delhi studio.

04ProductionProduction usually takes 6–8 weeks, depending on the design and specification. We confirm a delivery date after reviewing the specification.

Practicalities

Before you
brief us.

Which stones do you work with?
Malachite, lapis lazuli, rose quartz, crystal quartz, selenite, onyx and marble, alongside mother-of-pearl and abalone shell. Ask if you have another stone in mind.
Can you inlay our own design?
Yes. Send the drawing or CAD file. The stones and the cutting are resolved with you before the sample.
How large can a piece be?
It depends on the piece and the stone. We confirm the possible size, and any joins, at design stage.
Is there a sample?
Yes. A sample is made for your approval before production.
How long does it take?
Production usually takes 6–8 weeks, depending on the design and specification.
Do you install?
No. We make and supply; installation is carried out by your appointed installer or contractor.
Is there a minimum order?
No fixed minimum. One table top or a full scheme.
Who contracts and invoices?
Art Avenue Pvt. Ltd., New Delhi — the same company that contracts our rugs, embroidery and leather.
